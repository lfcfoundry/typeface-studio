What it is and how it works

Words in, font out

Type something like "a warm, friendly storybook font, not too heavy" and press Make my font. Press Try another for a different take.

  • It reads your words. It understands "not too heavy", "a bit bolder", "like Futura but friendlier", slanted and italic words, and themes such as typewriter, wedding invitation or sports brand. Every change it makes is listed with its reason, and each guess has an Undo.
  • It draws smooth letters. A pen engine draws all 95 printable ASCII characters in Sans, Serif or Slab, in Regular and Bold, with automatic spacing and kerning.
  • It exports real fonts. The outlines are compiled to TrueType files you can download and install.
  • You can fine-tune. Sliders for width, heights, stroke thickness and contrast, spacing, openness, squareness, stress angle and slant, plus a glyph grid and editor.
Why it exists

A font without learning a font editor

  • A personal or project font for people with no design skills: fewest steps from an idea to a file.
  • Private by design: no ads, no accounts, no network use while you work.
  • Quick experiments: try moods and styles in seconds before committing to a direction.
Honest limits

What it does not do yet

  • Printable ASCII only, not broad Unicode or other writing systems.
  • It is not a professional font editor. Curved letters carry many outline points, so editing single points by hand is awkward.
  • Looks have been checked by eye in Chromium, Firefox and WebKitGTK on a desktop. Phones are not tested yet.
  • Generated fonts are not hinted and have not had a professional quality review.
